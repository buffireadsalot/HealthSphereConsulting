Health Sphere Consulting
Website Site Plan

A minimal, quietly modern brand for healthcare technology consulting, with a flexible foundation for future software products.

Buffin Ancestry is a quality and usability reference—not a template. This site uses its own typographic, visual, and structural identity.

01 / Foundation

Site name, purpose & audiences

Site: Health Sphere Consulting · Domain: healthsphereconsulting.com

Purpose: Present established healthcare IT consulting expertise in EDI/X12, interoperability, implementations, practice management, and revenue-cycle operations, while introducing an Innovation Lab for emerging Python and AI-assisted products.

Audience: Community health centers and FQHCs, physician practices, healthcare technology vendors, implementation leaders, and potential collaborators.

Visitor scenarios

  • A practice manager needs help diagnosing persistent EDI rejection or reimbursement problems.
  • A health IT vendor seeks support for integration testing, workflow analysis, or implementation.
  • A collaborator wants to understand the future EDI Developer Workbench without assuming it is commercially available today.
  • A prospective client wants to review professional expertise and make contact.

03 / Typography & mark

Structured sans-serif typography

Montserrat: Logo wordmark, navigation, headings, and labels. Medium or semibold with controlled letter spacing.

Source Sans 3: Paragraphs, cards, forms, and longer explanations. Accessible line spacing and calm readability.

Logo concept: An incomplete geometric ring with a single accent point suggests interconnectedness and a sphere without resorting to a cross, heartbeat, or stereotypical AI graphic.

06 / Build & governance

Technical plan

  • Hosting: GitHub Pages, with existing custom domain configured when ready.
  • Code: Semantic HTML and CSS with minimal JavaScript for design-preview switching only.
  • Private files: Contracts, proposals, and internal documentation remain in OneDrive, not the public repository.
  • Email: Outlook address added later. Avoid publishing an unfinished inquiry form that cannot deliver messages.
  • Accessibility: Responsive layout, keyboard focus, semantic landmarks, readable contrast, and reduced-motion support.
  • Launch discipline: Describe AI work as experimental until validated; do not imply a finished commercial EDI product.
